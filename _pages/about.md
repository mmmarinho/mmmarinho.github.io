### Overview

I am a Lecturer in Robotics with the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, [The University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk), Manchester, UK, where I lead a group of five researchers. I am based at [RAICo (Robotics and AI Collaboration)](https://hotrobotics.co.uk/facilities/university-of-manchester-2/), Whitehaven.

I obtained a PhD (2018) in Mechanical Engineering from the [University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/index.html), where I was an Assistant Professor (助教) until late 2023. I have a BSc (2012) in Mechatronics Engineering and an MSc (2014) in Electronic Systems and Automation Engineering from the [University of Brasilia](http://international.unb.br/).

### Consulting

I take on consulting work through The University of Manchester, under the [University Consultancy Policy](https://documents.manchester.ac.uk/display.aspx?DocID=75531). Areas include:
- Robot control software for manipulators: kinematic modelling, constrained control, teleoperation, and multi-arm coordination.
- Systems integration: specification, procurement, electrical and electronic design, mechatronics, and software for control and image processing.
- ROS 2 software: architecture, drivers, packaging, and CI.
- Training in software for robotics (ROS 2) and whole-body control.

For enquiries, please email me at [murilo.marinho@manchester.ac.uk](mailto:murilo.marinho@manchester.ac.uk) with a short description and the expected timeline.

### Research 

<iframe width="290" height="160" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/videoseries?list=PLfnnpBCwI_l8DMpm5Q5t8gjXYFoM_tOlz" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>

My work has been focused on teleoperated and/or (semi-)autonomous robots applied to nuclear decommissioning, [medicine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dayuW47PKKc&list=TLGGLlmonU1w7aAyMjA5MjAyMQ) and [scientific exploration](https://aiscienceplatform.github.io). Notably
- Nuclear decommissioning: robot control and teleoperation at RAICo, with partners including Sellafield Ltd, UKAEA, NDA, and AWE.
- Paediatric surgery: [ICRA19](http://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2019.8794363), [RAL20](http://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2019.2963642), [T-MRB21](http://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1109/TMRB.2021.3049878), and [JMRCAS23](http://doi.org/10.1002/rcs.2476).
- Eye surgery: [T-MRB22](http://doi.org/10.1109/TMRB.2022.3147033), [ICRA23](http://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA48891.2023.10160795), and [IJMRCAS25](http://doi.org/10.1002/rcs.70040).
- Neurosurgery: [T-RO19](http://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2019.2920078) and [IJMRCAS20](http://doi.org/10.1002/rcs.2053).
- Scientific exploration: [IROS23](http://doi.org/10.1109/IROS55552.2023.10341693), [RAM24](http://doi.org/10.1109/MRA.2023.3336472), [ICRA24](http://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10610663), and [T-ASE25](http://doi.org/10.1109/TASE.2025.3630482).

Those implementations are based on a constrained kinematic control framework, used on manipulators, surgical robots, and multi-arm systems.
- (Adaptive) constrained kinematic control: [T-RO19](http://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2019.2920078) and [T-RO22](http://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2022.3181047). 
- Software implementation and system integration: [IJMRCAS20](http://doi.org/10.1002/rcs.2053), [RAM21](http://doi.org/10.1109/MRA.2020.2997920), and [RAM24](http://doi.org/10.1109/MRA.2023.3336472).
